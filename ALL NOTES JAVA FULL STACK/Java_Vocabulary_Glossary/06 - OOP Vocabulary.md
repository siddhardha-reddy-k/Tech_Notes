# 6. OOP Vocabulary

## Object-Oriented Programming

Programming based around objects containing state and behavior.

---

## Encapsulation

Keeping data and related behavior together while controlling access to internal state.

---

## Abstraction

Showing necessary behavior while hiding unnecessary implementation details.

---

## Inheritance

A class acquiring accessible behavior and characteristics from another class.

---

## Polymorphism

Allowing one parent/interface reference to represent different implementations.

---

## Association

A relationship between two independent classes.

---

## Aggregation

A weaker **HAS-A** relationship.

The related object can usually exist independently.

---

## Composition

A stronger **HAS-A** relationship.

The contained object's lifecycle is usually closely connected to the owner.

---

## IS-A Relationship

Represents inheritance.

Example:

```text
Dog IS-A Animal
```

---

## HAS-A Relationship

Represents association, aggregation, or composition.

Example:

```text
Car HAS-A Engine
```
